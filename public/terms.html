ILM API — TERMS

Terms and acceptable use

Short, because this is a small service with one purpose: making Quran, hadith and Islamic scholarship available to people building useful things. Using the API means accepting what is below. Last updated 9 November 2026.

The data, honestly

The Quran text, translations, tafsir, hadith and fiqh material are scholarly works carried from their published editions. They are reproduced for study and reference. They are not a fatwa, not a substitute for a qualified teacher, and not infallible: translations in particular differ, and a translation is never the Quran itself.

Not every printed number has a record here. Where the edition this data was seeded from carried a placeholder instead of text, the segment was removed rather than shown blank, so 375 of 47,457 narrations (203 of them in Sahih Muslim) have no entry. A missing number answers 404. Treat that as absent, not broken, and do not fill the gap with a narration from another edition: the numbering will not match, and a wrong citation is worse than none.

When you publish something built on this data, cite it as it was returned: collection, number and translator where given. Do not present generated or edited text as scripture.

Keys and limits

A free key allows 60 requests a minute and 2,000 a day, reported in the X-RateLimit headers and at GET /v1/usage. One key is for one developer or one application: do not share a key between people, and do not work around the limits by creating keys in bulk. Cache what you fetch; the content is immutable and re-fetching it costs you quota and nothing else.

Your key is yours to protect. If it leaks, replace it immediately with POST /v1/developers/keys/rotate using the key itself, or delete it with /keys/revoke. Lost keys are emailed to the address on file by POST /v1/developers/keys/recover.

What counts as misuse

Do not resell the raw API or a wholesale mirror of it as your own product, and do not bulk-scrape around the limits; if you need a full offline copy for research, ask, and we will find a sane way. Do not misrepresent the output as a ruling, a scholar's verdict or an official position. Do not use the API to harass, to incite, or to put words into the mouths of the sources. Do not attempt to break authentication, and do not probe other keys or accounts.

A key that is abusing the service is revoked without notice. Everything else gets a conversation first.

Your address

The email you sign up with is stored to issue your key and to send it to you if you lose it. It is not sold, not used for marketing, and not shared. Nothing else about you is collected; there are no tracking cookies on these pages.

Availability

The service is offered as it is, best effort, with no uptime guarantee and no liability for decisions made from its data. It may change: endpoints can be added, corrected or retired as the data improves. Breaking changes will be announced in the documentation rather than made silently.
